# Sky Islands 1.1.0 — Flight Club

## What to test

- Hold Climb and Speed together, then release only Speed. Climb should remain held.
- Pause while flying, approaching a runway, and parked. Resume after the three-second
  countdown. Switch apps or lock the phone, then return: the flight should remain paused.
- Open Settings from Flight Club and from Pause. Test sound, haptics, reduced motion,
  button size, button height and left/right-handed controls. Relaunch to check persistence.
- Try Training: hold Climb, collect the orange petrol drum, then approach a runway and
  tap Land. Training is invincible and awards no gold or mission progress.
- Complete today's three missions. Each reward enters the bank once and never adds to
  the ranked flight score. Missions reset at midnight UTC.
- Land to load a parcel, take off and deliver it at the named next runway. Missing that
  island cancels the parcel; a later landing starts another delivery. Each island pays
  its landing bonus only once per flight.
- Fly through gold rings and chain them with cloud bounces or low passes for a combo.
  Missing a ring, hitting terrain or waiting six seconds breaks the chain.
- Open the Logbook after finding islands and landmarks. Check unlocked landmarks,
  recent discoveries, achievement badges and completed daily mission badges.
- Compare all five aircraft and island themes in the Hangar and in flight. Aircraft now
  have different silhouettes, surface details and landing gear. Biomes add palms,
  snow/ice, lava vents, lights, landmarks and waterfalls, with cached cliff textures.
- Daily Race lasts up to three minutes and always uses Bluebird and the day's shared
  theme. Saved invincibility and equipment do not affect this mode. Finish or end the
  flight and opt in to Post score. Choose My best ghost, Nearby rival or No ghost on
  the briefing screen, then start another race.
- Disconnect networking. Free Flight, training, missions and local personal ghosts
  should continue to work. Daily Race keeps its result locally; posting can be retried
  from the results screen after connectivity returns.
- Check notched iPhones, landscape in both directions, sound after Take off, the silent
  switch, readability and sustained frame rate on a real device. Linux checks cannot
  verify native audio behaviour, haptics or iOS lifecycle behaviour.

## Review information for a future 1.1.0 submission

No login, ads, purchases or subscriptions. Daily Race is optional online competition.
The Free Flight Secret code field accepts FLAMECAT (all planes/themes + invincibility),
IRONWING (toggle invincibility), and FULLTANK (+2,500 in-game gold). These are built-in,
free, offline codes. FLAMECAT and FULLTANK can be used once per saved game. They are not
payment codes. Daily Race always disables invincibility, uses Bluebird, and posts to a
separate leaderboard. Free Flight, assisted flights and training cannot post race scores.

The old 1.0.1 (8) App Review clarification is recorded separately in metadata.md. Its
reply was saved as a draft, not sent. This TestFlight release does not replace that
App Store submission automatically.
