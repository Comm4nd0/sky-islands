How to play

  • ▲ climbs, ▶ speeds up, ◀ slows down. Let go of ▲ to sink.
  • Petrol burns as you fly. Grab petrol drums in the air, or tap LAND over a runway to refuel.
  • Rocks, hills and the sea cost a flame. You start with three.
  • Red jump pads and sparkly clouds bounce you into the thin air, where gold pays double.
  • Spend gold in the hangar on new planes and island skins.

Daily course and ghosts

Every pilot flies the same course each day (it changes at midnight UTC). Post your score from the game-over card and the day’s best runs fly beside everyone as ghosts.

Common questions

Will I lose my gold if I delete the app? Yes. Progress is saved on your phone only.

Does it work offline? Yes. Only the leaderboard and ghosts need a connection.

No sound? Sky Islands follows the silent switch. Flip it off and turn the volume up.

Someone has an offensive name. Email us the name and the day you saw it and we’ll remove it.

Remove my scores. Email us your pilot name and a recent score. See the privacy policy.
